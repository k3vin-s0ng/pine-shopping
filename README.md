This project is called Pine: Shopping. Your one stop shop for all your shopping and fashion needs. Our UI integrates an AI assistant that helps with both styling and shopping advice using a RAG approach on different product combinations. 

Our AI assistant is voice activated allowing you to hold a full conversation without the need to keep pressing the talk button (kind of like Siri). It'll ask you for details and the type of product you're trying to look for and when it's all ready you'll be linked directly to the products of your choosing. 

Dataset is not public but you can try it out at pineshopping.com

Feel free to fork or add on to it!
